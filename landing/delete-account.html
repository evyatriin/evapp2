How to delete your account

You can delete your EVraahein account at any time using either of the methods below. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Option 1 — Delete directly from the app

1Open the EVraahein app and sign in.
2Tap the Profile tab (bottom navigation).
3Tap Settings (gear icon, top right).
4Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
5Confirm the action. Your account and data will be deleted immediately.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you no longer have access to the app, email us from the address registered to your account:

hey@evraahein.app

Subject: Account Deletion Request
Include the email address or phone number on your account. We will process the request within 7 days.

Deletion is irreversible. Once your account is deleted, your data cannot be recovered. If you only want a break, you can simply sign out — your account will remain intact until you return.

How long does deletion take?

In-app deletion is immediate — your account and all associated data listed above is removed from active systems as soon as you confirm.

Email requests are processed within 7 days. You will receive a confirmation email once the deletion is complete.

Backups are purged on our normal backup rotation cycle (maximum 30 days). Anonymous, aggregated analytics are not purged as they cannot be linked back to you.
