Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 March 2026  ·  TaruniLakshmi Ventures, Hyderabad, India

1 Overview

At EVraahein, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how TaruniLakshmi Ventures collects, uses, shares, and protects your personal data when you use the EVraahein app or website.

We comply with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA) of India. By using EVraahein, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

We collect only what is necessary to provide and improve the Service:

3 How We Use Your Data

We use your data to:

  • Provide, operate, and improve the EVraahein Service
  • Calculate personalised EV routes and identify optimal charging stops
  • Send important service notifications (e.g., charging alerts, account activity)
  • Respond to support requests and queries
  • Analyse aggregate usage trends to improve the app
  • Send launch and product update emails (only if you signed up for early access)
  • Comply with legal obligations

We will never sell your personal data to third parties.

4 Location Data

EVraahein is a navigation service — precise location access is essential for route planning and charger discovery. Here is how we handle it:

  • Foreground only: We access location only while the app is open and in use
  • No background tracking: We do not track your location when the app is closed
  • Not sold: Your location data is never sold or shared with advertisers
  • Deletable: You can delete all stored trip history from your account settings

You may deny location permission, but core navigation features will not function without it.

5 Data Sharing

We share your data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: Trusted vendors who help us operate the app (cloud hosting, mapping APIs, analytics) — bound by strict data processing agreements
  • Legal compliance: When required by law, court order, or governmental authority in India
  • Business transfer: In the event of a merger or acquisition, your data may transfer to the new entity under equivalent privacy protections
  • With your consent: Any other sharing requires your explicit permission

6 Data Retention

We retain your personal data only as long as necessary:

  • Account data: Retained while your account is active; deleted within 30 days of account closure
  • Trip history: Retained for 12 months; you can delete it sooner from settings
  • Waitlist emails: Retained until the app launches or until you unsubscribe
  • Legal records: Some data may be retained longer where required by Indian law

8 Data Security

We implement industry-standard safeguards to protect your data:

  • All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using TLS 1.3
  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt — we never store plaintext passwords
  • Access to production systems is restricted to authorised personnel only
  • We conduct regular security reviews of our infrastructure

No system is 100% secure. In the unlikely event of a data breach affecting your rights, we will notify you within 72 hours of becoming aware, as required by law.

9 Children's Privacy

EVraahein is not directed at children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a minor, please contact us immediately at hey@evraahein.app and we will delete it promptly.

10 Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses minimal cookies for:

  • Essential functionality: Keeping you logged into the web portal
  • Analytics: Anonymised, aggregate traffic statistics (no cross-site tracking)

We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking pixels. You can disable cookies in your browser settings; this may affect some website features.

11 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our Service evolves or as regulations change. When we make material changes, we will notify you via email or an in-app notice at least 7 days before the changes take effect. The "Effective date" at the top of this page always reflects the latest version.
