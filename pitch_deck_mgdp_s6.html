The Problem

Every Indian EV Owner Faces
Three Invisible Walls

Adoption is soaring — but the ownership experience still runs on anxiety
1
Range Anxiety — "Will I make it?"
Dashboard SOC percentages are theoretical. Real-world range depends on traffic, elevation, AC load, battery age — none of which the factory display accounts for. 63% of Indian EV owners report anxiety about running out of charge on every long journey.
2
Charging Desert — "Where do I charge?"
India has fewer than 25,000 public EV chargers for 3 million+ EVs. Existing apps show stale data, unmaintained stations, and no real-time availability. Drivers waste 45+ minutes per trip hunting for working chargers.
3
Battery Mystery — "Is my battery healthy?"
OEMs provide no consumer-facing battery health tool. State of Health (SOH) is locked inside diagnostic ECUs. Owners discover degradation only when range drops 30% — often after the warranty window. There is no early warning system.

Live Battery Metrics via UDS

  • State of Charge (SOC) — raw ECU value

  • Pack Voltage — live HV bus reading
  • Battery Current (A) — charge/discharge
  • Cell Voltage Delta (mV) — imbalance warning
  • Coolant / Battery Temperature

ECU Headers

RX: 0x789
Protocol: ISO-TP (15765-2)
Mode: UDS 0x22 (Read Data By ID)
